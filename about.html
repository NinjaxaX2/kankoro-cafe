About us

Kankoro Cafe

A neighborhood cafe experience designed for coffee lovers and food enthusiasts. We believe in quality, community, and creating moments worth remembering.

Our Story

Crafted with care.

Kankoro Cafe was born from a simple idea: create a space where quality coffee, fresh food, and genuine connection come together. Located in the heart of CR Park Market, we've built something special—a neighborhood destination where every visit feels like coming home.

Our coffee is sourced from premium plantations and roasted in-house to perfection. Our menu celebrates vegetarian cuisine with bold flavors and thoughtful ingredients. And our space? It's designed for lingering—for conversations, work, meetings, or simply enjoying a moment of peace.

We believe great cafe experiences aren't just about the product—they're about the people, the atmosphere, and the feeling of being part of something community-driven.
